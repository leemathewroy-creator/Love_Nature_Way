Bohol Chocolate Hills

The Chocolate Hills are a unique geological formation in Bohol, Philippines. They are covered in grass that turns brown during the dry season, creating a landscape that resembles chocolate mounds.

I recommend Bohol for its clean, fresh air and good atmosphere. It is a great place for capturing aesthetic Instagram photos, especially during golden hour and around the Chocolate Hills.


Bataan

Las Casas Filipinas de Acuzar is a historical site in Bataan, Philippines. It features traditional Filipino architecture and offers insights into the region's history.

I recommend Las Casas Filipinas de Acuzar for its historical significance and photo-friendly spots. It is a great place for Instagram photos, especially architectural shots, cultural images, and evening pictures.


Davao City

Davao City is a vibrant metropolis in Mindanao, known for its lush greenery, bustling markets, and diverse cultural scene. It offers a unique blend of modern amenities and natural beauty.

I recommend Davao City for its rich culture and photo opportunities. It is a great place for Instagram photos, especially the fresh air, food photography, and nature images.
